Evidence

How to read the checks

  • Delay test: a genuine signal loses performance gradually when trades are delayed. A collapse after one extra day means the backtest used information it would not have had.
  • Costs: the break-even cost is the trading cost at which the edge disappears. High turnover makes small edges fragile.
  • Deflated Sharpe: adjusts for how many variants were tried. The more variants, the higher the bar luck alone could clear.
  • Exposure and concentration: checks the book against the mandate's leverage and position limits, and whether a few names or days produce most of the result.
  • Time windows: a reliable edge should be positive in most periods, not concentrated in one.

Method and reproducibility

  • Weights are decided at the close of each date using only information available then, and earn the next period's return.
